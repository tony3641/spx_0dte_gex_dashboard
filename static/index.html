Simulation report

Each row in the sweep table is one (SL ×, k) configuration; the charts below show the selected row's outcome distribution. Hover the “?” circles — next to every control and chart — for plain-language explanations.

Day PnL distribution ? The spread of simulated per-day profit & loss across all paths. Each bar is a $ bucket; bars to the right of $0 are winning days, bars to the left are losing days. A long tail to the left means the strategy occasionally takes a big loss. The tiles above (mean, win rate, CVaR) read off this distribution.
Spread mark-to-market (MTM) through the day ? Each line is a quantile of the open spread's unrealized P&L at that time of day, across all paths. The shaded band is the 5th–95th percentile: 90% of paths sit between the two edges. The gold line is the median (a typical day). A band that dips well below $0 early on means the position is commonly in drawdown before it resolves — a sign of a stressful hold.
Bootstrap max drawdown ? Drawdown is the biggest peak-to-trough loss on an equity curve. This chart resamples the simulated day PnLs into many 60-day sequences and plots each sequence's worst drawdown. Ruin probability is the share of sequences that ever draw down past your equity × threshold. A peak on the right means a real chance of a deep multi-day loss.
